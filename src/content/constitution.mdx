## What the constitution covers

The SGA constitution is the governing document for the Rockville Student Government
Association. It defines:

- The purpose and powers of the SGA
- Officer positions (President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer) and their duties
- Senator-at-Large eligibility, election, and term
- Standing committees, including the Budget Panel and Ethics Committee
- Meeting rules, quorum, and voting
- Attendance and accountability standards
- How the constitution is amended

## How to get a copy

Ask at the Monday open meeting or email [sgarv@montgomerycollege.edu](mailto:sgarv@montgomerycollege.edu).
The Office of Student Life (SV 104) also keeps the current version on file.
